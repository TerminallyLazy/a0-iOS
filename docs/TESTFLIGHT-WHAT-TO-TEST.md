Build 9: Choose model presets before your first message

• Open the app into New chat and choose a model preset immediately. The composer should show your choice without opening an old chat or sending a message first.
• Send the first message and confirm it uses the chosen preset. Try Use inherited before sending, then try switching presets in an existing chat.
• Choose a preset, switch to another chat and back, or relaunch the app. Your unsent preset choice and draft should stay together for the same account.
• If applying a preset fails or the connection is interrupted, the message should remain unsent. Confirm the outcome and explicitly choose a preset or inheritance before retrying; interrupted requests must not replay automatically.

Also check shared preset editing, normal chat, attachments, and reconnect behavior. Report device/iOS version and reproduction steps; do not include credentials.
